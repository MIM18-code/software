# Video walkthroughs

These recordings use fictional Night Ferry material in the running app, in light mode at 1440x900. Each H.264 MP4 has burned captions and no audio. The SRT provides separate captions. The narration files are macOS `say` drafts, timed to the caption cues, for reviewing the wording before a final voice recording.

AI, Tailscale and table-read recordings demonstrate setup and controls. They stop before account sign-in, model or voice downloads, a second Mac connecting, or voice playback. The transcript walkthrough shows a real local formatting preview in the Dictate panel and inserts the result; microphone dictation was not tested. The Movie Magic example writes a real export using a scripted native save dialog. The tagging and handoff example applies a real Prop tag and imports the reviewed draft into an isolated Backlot project. Its native project chooser and confirmation are supplied by the recording harness.

The AI setup and co-writing examples use synthetic runtime and network status to show first-use controls without installing tools or exposing account information. They do not prove a download or remote connection.

The editor overview was captured on beta 7. The AI setup and co-writing clips show the newer development screens. The remaining recordings were captured on beta 8. The guide explains the earlier setup buttons in the public beta 8 release as well.

Captured on November 2, 2026. The downloadable [Fountain](../sample/Night-Ferry.fountain) and [tagged FDX](../sample/Night-Ferry-tagged.fdx) samples contain six scenes. Backlot screenshots use a separate demonstration project with additional fictional scenes.
