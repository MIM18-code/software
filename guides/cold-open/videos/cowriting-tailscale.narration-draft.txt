Co-writing on different networks.

Open the Co-writing panel for the sample draft.

Choose Share for Co-writing to start a local session.

Choose where the co-writer is located.

For another network, choose On a different network.

The panel explains how to connect both Macs with Tailscale.

Check Tailscale again after connecting; an invite appears when ready.

Stop Sharing when the session is no longer needed.
