Writers Block Screenplay dictation for Final Draft that runs on your Mac

Click an empty paragraph in Final Draft, press Command-Option-D and talk through the scene: where you are, what happens, who says what. Press it again and the scene lands at your cursor as real Final Draft paragraphs, with the scene heading, action, character names, parentheticals and dialogue each in its own element. The speech recognition and the formatting both run on your Mac.

The formatting model gets most takes right, not every one. Read what it pasted the way you'd read any dictated draft. Every recording and transcript is saved, so a take that goes wrong can be fixed or run again.

What it does

What the model does
The model decides which of your sentences is action and which is dialogue. It isn't asked to rewrite them. When a take drops or changes words, Writers Block pastes it anyway and tells you which words to check.
Corrections
“Scratch that”, “no wait” and corrections partway through a sentence are handled. So is a heading you say in the middle of a sentence, like “she walks in, interior kitchen, night”.
Where it runs
Parakeet transcribes your recording and a writing model running in Ollama formats it, both on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded. On first launch, Writers Block walks you through installing Ollama and downloading the two models, about 6 GB in all.
Other writing apps
In the Writers Block workspace you can record or paste a transcript, check the scene, then copy it, send it to any open app, or export Fountain, FDX or plain text.
Other dictation apps
If you already dictate with another app, paste its text into the workspace and Writers Block formats it the same way.

Questions

Do I need Final Draft?

No. Writers Block pastes into Final Draft if you have it, and its own workspace exports Fountain, FDX or plain text for any other writing app.

Is my voice sent anywhere?

No. The speech recognition and the formatting model both run on your Mac.

How much disk space does it need?

About 8 GB free. The two models it downloads on first launch are about 6 GB together.

Install

Open the disk image and drag Writers Block to Applications. Needs an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later and about 8 GB free for the models. On first launch it asks for the microphone, and for Accessibility so it can paste into Final Draft. It also helps you install Ollama, the free app that runs the writing model.
